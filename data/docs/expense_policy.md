# Travel and Business Expense Policy

## Travel Reimbursement
All business-related travel must be pre-approved by the respective department head. Flights should be booked under economy class unless exceptional circumstances apply.

## Accommodation & Hotel Limits
For business travel, hotel expenses are reimbursed up to specific limits. In metro cities, the maximum allowed hotel per night is 6,000 rupees. For non-metro cities, the limit is 3,500 rupees per night.

## Approval Hierarchy
- Expenses up to 10,000 rupees require manager approval.
- Expenses between 10,000 and 50,000 rupees require approval from the Department Head or Director.
- Who approves an expense of 20,000 rupees? An expense of 20,000 rupees must be approved by the Department Head.
- Any expense exceeding 50,000 rupees requires VP or Finance Controller sign-off.
