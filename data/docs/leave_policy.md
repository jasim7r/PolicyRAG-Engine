# Annual Leave and Sick Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused leave days to the following year; any additional unused days expire.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per year. If sick leave extends beyond 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate signed by a licensed physician must be submitted upon return to work.

## Public Holidays
The company observes standard national public holidays as notified annually by HR.
