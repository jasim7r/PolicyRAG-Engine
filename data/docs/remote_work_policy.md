# Remote Work and IT Security Policy

## Hybrid Schedule
Full-time employees operate on a hybrid schedule. Employees work remotely on Mondays and Fridays. Which days do I work from the office? Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are designated in-office working days.

## IT Security & Network Access
When working remotely, employees must connect via the corporate VPN at all times. Connecting directly to unsecured public wifi networks without activating the VPN is strictly prohibited to safeguard confidential customer and organizational data. Company laptops must not be shared with third parties.
